### FAQ

**Do you charge the same for a website and an internal tool?**
No. A website is a display layer: pages, content, and forms. An internal tool has backend logic, data structure, user roles, and workflows behind it. That backend work is what moves the price, not how the tool looks on screen.

**Can you quote a website and an internal system together?**
Yes. We map both into one build plan and one combined quote instead of treating them as separate projects.

**Are the prices fixed?**
Every project gets a fixed quote before work starts. The numbers on this page are starting points so you can judge fit. The final number comes from a short scope review, not a formula.

**Do I need a technical brief before contacting you?**
No. Tell us the problem you're trying to solve or the thing you want to build. We'll ask the technical questions.

**Do you offer support after launch?**
Yes. Support is optional and scoped separately from the build, so you're not locked into a retainer you don't need.

**Can you help with SEO-ready websites?**
Yes. Technical SEO foundations, like page structure, speed, and metadata, are built into every website tier. Deeper content optimization is available as an add-on.

**How does AI factor into what you build?**
Two ways. First, AI can be a feature inside your project: summarizing inbound messages, extracting data from documents, drafting first-pass responses, or tagging and routing records, always with a human review step. Second, we use an AI-assisted development workflow ourselves, which lets us move through scaffolding, iteration, and testing faster. Every build is still reviewed, tested, and owned by a developer before it ships. The first is something you use. The second is how we keep your price lower without cutting quality.

1. **Hero headline.** Live `main.html` H1 reads "Not just a website. A system that runs your business." New hero is "We Build Digital Systems That Help Businesses Grow." Both are on-brand, but the new one is the one to ship; retire the old line everywhere it's reused (meta description, OG tags).

2. **Pricing model replaced entirely.** Live `main.html` pricing section (`#pricing`, lines ~2500–2540) offers two cards: "Digital Growth Partner" (From $1,599/month retainer) and "Custom Build" (unpriced, "Scoped to your project"). This is a two-tier, largely-unpriced retainer model. It is fully replaced by the three-category, nine-tier, published-band structure in Section 1 above. The $1,599/month retainer offer has no equivalent in the new architecture and needs an explicit decision: fold it into Website Management, drop it, or keep it as a separate retainer product outside these three categories.

These need to update to reflect the new $500 anchor and per-tier bands, or search engines and rich results will show numbers that contradict the visible page.

4. **FAQ answers are stale.** The live FAQ ("What's the investment for a new website?") states "$1,999 to $4,999" for business websites and refers to "Larger custom platforms... quoted individually." This entire answer is superseded by the new published-band FAQ in Section 1, which states not-charged-the-same, combined-scope, and fixed-quote answers explicitly.

5. **Website Management price is retained.** $299/month is unchanged and consistent between the old site and the new copy. No conflict here, just confirm this line survives the rewrite.

6. **Google Ads Management ($300/month + spend) has no home in the new architecture.** It doesn't fit Websites, Internal Tools, or Automation/AI cleanly. Needs a decision: keep as a separate recurring line alongside Website Management, or drop it from the public service list.

The new copy sorts all of these into exactly three categories plus a management/SEO/ads line that sits outside them (see item 6). The schema's `itemListElement` needs restructuring to mirror the three-category architecture.

8. **No existing service landing pages exist to redirect from or supersede.** The six new pages (Custom App & Internal Tool Development, High-Performance Website Development, Workflow Automation & Integration Services, Practical AI App Development, Conversion Landing Page Development, MVP Development for Startups) are net-new URLs, not rewrites of existing pages. Nothing to redirect, but nav and sitemap need new entries.

9. **AI is not mentioned anywhere in the current live copy.** This is a net-new introduction, not a rewrite, so there's no old AI framing to contradict or walk back.

10. **"Agency" appears only in a third-party script tag** (`designrush.com/.../agency-reviews.js`), not in visible copy referring to Aequora itself. No violation of the "agency" ban found in current body copy; leave the DesignRush widget as-is since it's an external listing name, not self-description.
