About Jordan

Helping small businesses scale smart since 2017.

My approach is direct, data-driven, and built for the real world — not theory. Together we identify what's slowing you down, build systems to fix it, and set targets you can actually hit.

Whether you're stuck at six figures or scaling past a million, I'll give you a clear roadmap and hold you accountable every step of the way.
