DC Miranda

Founder & Developer, Aequora Digital

I'm DC Miranda, the founder and developer behind Aequora Digital.

I spent years building and managing websites for small businesses, landing pages, membership sites, full builds, mostly on tools like WordPress, Squarespace, and page builders. I worked with business owners day to day, handling the design, the updates, the automations, the parts that keep a site running.

Over time I kept noticing the same problem. Businesses were handed a website that looked fine but did nothing else. It sat there like a brochure. The moment they needed it to actually do something, book an appointment, keep records, follow up with a customer, it fell short, and they were back to juggling spreadsheets and manual work.

So I built Aequora to do it properly. I build the website your customers see and the tools that run the work behind it, made to fit the way your business already operates.

We built Aequora Digital because too many small business owners get stuck with template sites that break or large firms that take the deposit and hand the work to someone they never meet. Neither leaves you with something that actually runs your business. We wanted to do it the honest way, where the people who understand your business are the same ones who build it.

That is how we work. We help small businesses get modern websites and simple software that save time, look right, and keep working long after launch. Fewer clients, done properly, is the whole point.

One person, accountable, building things that make running your business easier.
